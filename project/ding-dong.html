BEHIND THE SCENES

What it is: the fan favorite. An abandoned plantation house deep in the swamp, one red window lit, fog crawling over black water. Swamp gothic, full stop.

How it was made: location-feel built from scratch — every board on that house, every strand of Spanish moss placed on purpose. The red window is the only warm light in the whole piece, and it carries the entire video.

The detail that matters: the half-sunk rowboat at the dock. Nobody comments on it, everybody feels it.
