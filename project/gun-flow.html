BEHIND THE SCENES

What it is: a studio session cut — fast, raw, no polish where it doesn't belong. Rain-slick streets, red taillights, steam off the asphalt.

How it was made: edited to the tempo, not the other way around. The cuts land on the beat and the camera never sits still for long. Wet-street reflections did most of the lighting work — sometimes the location hands you the look for free.

The detail that matters: the long shadow. One figure, stretched down an empty street — that's the whole song in one frame.
