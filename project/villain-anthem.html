BEHIND THE SCENES

What it is: the official visualizer for Villain Anthem — a throne-room piece built around red moonlight, stone, and smoke. The heaviest visual the studio's put out so far.

How it was made: cut and colored in-house, frame by frame. The throne-room look came from stacking practical-feeling textures — cracked stone, drifting fog, ember light — over the performance. No stock templates, no plug-and-play presets.

The detail that matters: the red rim-light on the figure. That's the studio signature now — if you see it, you know where it came from.
