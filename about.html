ABOUT THE STUDIO

I'm Dustin. I run Dustinnope Animation Studios out of Lockport, Louisiana — population small, swamp big.

I'm a rapper and songwriter first. The animation studio exists because my songs needed visuals and I wasn't about to hand that to somebody who doesn't hear the music the way I do. So I learned the craft and built the whole thing myself — the songs, the videos, the artwork, all of it.

No label. No studio suits. No committee watering things down. I write it, I cut it, I color it, I own every frame outright. What you see on this site is one man's work, start to finish.

The wolf's the mark for a reason. It runs alone, it hunts honest, and it doesn't beg for a seat at anybody's table. That's how I make music and that's how I make videos. If you want pretty and polished for the algorithm, keep scrolling — this ain't that.
